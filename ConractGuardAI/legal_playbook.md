# Corporate Legal Negotiation Playbook (Standard Vendor NDA & MSA Policy)

## Rule 1: Limitation of Liability Cap
- **Policy:** Vendor liability must NEVER be uncapped or limited only to 1 month of fees. Standard liability cap must be at least 12 months of fees paid or $100,000 (whichever is greater).
- **Approved Redline Wording:** "Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of twelve (12) months' fees paid or payable hereunder or USD $100,000."

## Rule 2: Intellectual Property (IP) Ownership
- **Policy:** All custom deliverables, code, and work product created for the Company must belong exclusively to the Company ("Work Made for Hire"). Vendors may retain only pre-existing background IP.
- **Approved Redline Wording:** "All Deliverables and Work Product created specifically for Company shall be the sole and exclusive property of Company upon creation. Vendor retains ownership solely of its Pre-Existing IP."

## Rule 3: Governing Law & Jurisdiction
- **Policy:** Governing law must be India (Bengaluru, Karnataka) or Delaware/New York. Unilateral foreign arbitration in high-cost jurisdictions (e.g., London LCIA or Cayman Islands) is prohibited.
- **Approved Redline Wording:** "This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction."

## Rule 4: Auto-Renewal & Termination Notice
- **Policy:** Contracts must not auto-renew for multi-year terms without 30 days' prior written notice, and Company must have termination for convenience with 30 days' notice.
- **Approved Redline Wording:** "Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice without penalty. Any renewal requires mutual written consent."

## Rule 5: Confidentiality & Data Privacy (Responsible AI / Security)
- **Policy:** Vendor must not use Company confidential data to train public AI models and must notify Company of any data breach within 24 hours.
- **Approved Redline Wording:** "Vendor shall not use Company Confidential Information to train any machine learning or AI models and shall notify Company within twenty-four (24) hours of any suspected security breach."
